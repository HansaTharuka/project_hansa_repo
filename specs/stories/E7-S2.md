# E7-S2: Goal management service

**Epic:** E7 — Goals & Progress Tracking
**Layer:** Service
**Group:** D
**Depends on:** E7-S1

## Description

Implement create/edit logic for customer goals including validation of target_amount, target_date, and priority.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Creating a goal with target_amount <= 0 is rejected with a validation error.
2. Creating a goal with a target_date in the past is rejected with a validation error.
3. A customer can hold more than one goal simultaneously; creating a second goal does not remove or overwrite the first.
4. Editing an existing goal's priority succeeds and is reflected on the next read, without altering created_at.
5. A customer cannot create or edit a goal belonging to a different customer_id; the service enforces an ownership check.
